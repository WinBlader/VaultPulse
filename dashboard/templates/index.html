🎯 PROBLEM & OBJECTIVE

Solving Fixed-Schedule ATM Inefficiencies with Big Data

Traditional bank teams refill ATMs on fixed schedules. This creates two huge problems: High-traffic ATMs run out of cash (Stockouts), frustrating customers, while low-traffic ATMs sit with idle excess money, locking up bank capital. Our solution streams withdrawals through Kafka, stores them on HDFS, and uses a Time-Series ML Model to predict next-day cash demand so cash vans only deliver what is needed.
