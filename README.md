Project Overview & Problem Statement❌ The Industry Problem: Fixed-Schedule RefillsCommercial bank cash operations teams traditionally replenish ATMs on a blind, fixed schedule (e.g., loading a flat $35,000 into every ATM every Monday morning). This rigid schedule completely ignores localized demand patterns and causes two severe operational failures:Cash-Outs / Stockouts at High-Traffic Machines: ATMs located at transit hubs, shopping malls, and airports experience surges in customer cash withdrawals. Under a flat schedule, these machines run dry within hours, causing customer dissatisfaction, lost interchange fee revenue, and severe bank SLA penalty fines.Idle Excess Cash at Low-Traffic Machines: ATMs in quiet residential neighborhoods receive the same $35,000 refill despite dispensing only $8,000 to $10,000 per week. Tens of thousands of dollars sit idle inside vault cassettes earning 0% interest, incurring vault insurance premiums, and locking up liquid bank reserves that could otherwise generate yield.💡 The Solution: VaultPulse AIVaultPulse AI replaces blind fixed schedules with an automated, closed-loop Big Data Analytics & Machine Learning pipeline:Real-Time Streaming: Captures customer cash withdrawals in real time via Apache Kafka (atm-withdrawal-transactions).Big Data Storage: Persists and partitions transaction streams inside Hadoop HDFS (/atm_transactions/year=YYYY/month=MM/day=DD/).Time-Series ML Forecasting: Automatically trains a Random Forest Regressor over historical HDFS partitions using 14 lag and calendar features to predict tomorrow's cash demand per ATM.Smart Replenishment Optimizer: Adds a dynamic 15% safety stock buffer, classifies machine risk (STOCKOUT_ALERT, EXCESS_CASH_ALERT, OPTIMAL), and recommends the exact replenishment cash to load into armored delivery vans.Docker Multi-Container Cluster: Fully orchestrated in Docker Compose running 5 independent services (Zookeeper, Kafka, Hadoop NameNode, Hadoop DataNode, and VaultPulse App).Interactive Operations Dashboard: An asynchronous, glassmorphism web console with live Chart.js demand curves, real-time WebSocket transaction tickers, one-click Cash Van dispatch, dynamic HDFS partition ingestion, and model retraining.📖 2. End-to-End Operational PipelineLive Transaction Ingestion (Kafka):Customer withdrawals are pushed to Kafka topic atm-withdrawal-transactions. The producer decrements the physical vault balance of the machine in real time.Distributed Lake Storage (HDFS):The consumer batches incoming transaction streams into date partitions (/atm_transactions/year=YYYY/month=MM/day=DD/part-XXXX.csv) and updates the NameNode hdfs_namenode_fsimage.json metadata catalog.Feature Engineering & ML Training:Historical records from HDFS are loaded and enriched with 14 time-series lag and seasonal features. The Random Forest model trains over completed days, learning weekday patterns, weekend shopping spikes, and holiday effects.Replenishment Optimization Math:For each ATM $i$ for day $t+1$:$$\text{Target Cash Level}_{i, t+1} = \widehat{D}_{i, t+1} \times 1.15 \quad (\text{Includes } 15\% \text{ Safety Buffer})$$$$\text{Recommended Cash}_{i, t+1} = \max\Big(0, \, \min\big(\text{Max Capacity}_i - C_{i, t}, \, \text{Target Cash Level}_{i, t+1} - C_{i, t}\big)\Big)$$Where $\widehat{D}_{i, t+1}$ is predicted demand, and $C_{i, t}$ is current vault cash.Risk Classification Rules:
$$\text{Risk Status} = \begin{cases} \text{\textbf{STOCKOUT\_ALERT (Red)}} & \text{if } C_{i, t} < \widehat{D}_{i, t+1} \\ \text{\textbf{EXCESS\_CASH\_ALERT (Yellow)}} & \text{if } C_{i, t} > 2.2 \times \text{Target Cash Level}_{i, t+1} \\ \text{\textbf{OPTIMAL (Green)}} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$Interactive UI & Real-Time Visualization:The browser establishes a persistent WebSocket connection to /ws/transactions. Incoming transactions update the live terminal, decrement the live cash column in the table, and trigger visual warning pulses.💻 6.
